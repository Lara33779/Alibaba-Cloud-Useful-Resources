### Security Center
- **Feature**: Security Center Supports Unified Billing and Quota Sharing Across Accounts (2025-05-22)  
- **Customer Value**: The feature targets users of Landing Zone who are managing multiple accounts and seeking centralized quota management, including the capability to share quotas across accounts. When resource usage in other accounts fluctuates, these users aim to consolidate payment under a single account for streamlined operations.

### Cloud Firewall
- **Feature**: Cloud Firewalls Are in General Availability in More Overseas Regions  
  - NAT Firewall is available in the **Philippines (Manila)** Region (2025-05-29)  
  - Internet Firewall is available in the **Mexico** Region (2025-06-05)  
  - NAT Firewall is available in the **Indonesia (Jakarta)** Region (2025-06-06)  
- **Customer Value**: Target enterprises and institutions that want to protect traffic of Internet-facing or NAT gateway assets will have the Cloud Firewalls protected in the above-supported regions.  
- **Document**: [Cloud Firewall Supported Regions - Alibaba Cloud](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/cloud-firewall/cloudfirewall/product-overview/supported-regions?utm_content=g_1000404796)

### ID Verification
- **Feature**: ID Verification Is Generally Available in the Singapore Region (2025-04-29)  
- **Customer Value**: ID Verification is available in the Singapore region, which helps with Know Your Customer services and reduces security risks resulting from cross-border data transfer in the China (Hong Kong) region.
