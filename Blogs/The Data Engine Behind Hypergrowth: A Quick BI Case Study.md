## Problem Statement: 

Firm Z’s rapid expansion exposed data fragmentation across its supply chain, manufacturing, and DTC sales, causing decision delays and inefficiencies. Legacy systems failed to provide real-time insights, risking production bottlenecks and missed delivery targets. The company required a scalable BI solution to integrate siloed data, automate OTD (Order-to-Delivery) tracking, and align cross-functional teams—critical to maintaining its industry-leading innovation speed and IPO success.

## Solution Implemented:

Firm Z partnered with Quick BI to implement its "Z BI" data platform, creating a unified system for enterprise-wide analytics. The solution established real-time dashboards for comprehensive OTD (Order-to-Delivery) monitoring, giving leadership instant visibility into sales, production and logistics performance. It broke down departmental silos by creating seamless data flows between R&D, manufacturing, and sales operations while introducing automated KPI tracking across all business functions. 

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/72684543-f590-4545-b8bf-0c6667c16cef)

The platform evolved from initial marketing analytics to encompass full business intelligence capabilities, including financial, HR and R&D metrics. This rapid digital transformation enabled Firm Z to replace manual processes with data-driven decision making in record time, perfectly supporting the company's aggressive growth trajectory.

## Outcomes Achieved: 

The Quick BI implementation delivered exceptional business results for Firm Z, driving over 60% executive adoption of the Jishu BI platform while dramatically reducing decision latency. The company achieved industry-leading product launch speeds, delivering 10,000 car units in just 110 days.

Operational efficiency improved significantly through complete production-sales integration and reliable on-time delivery performance. This comprehensive digital transformation across supply chain, finance and R&D operations became a key enabler of Firm Z's record-breaking journey to IPO, cementing its reputation as an innovative EV market leader.
